outreach

below are some of the outreach activities I have taken part in to make science more accessible to everyone!

Magnetosphere, Ionosphere, Solar-Terrestrial (MIST)

Since 2025, I have been the PhD Student Representative for MIST, helping to create a supportive and inclusive environment for PhD students. I run the community’s social media, help organise our monthly online seminars, and contribute to yearly conferences such as Autumn MIST. Find out more about MIST and what we do.

Lancaster University

I have contributed to outreach activities with the School of Physics and Astronomy, including a public engagement event during the solar eclipse, where I showed members of the public how to observe the eclipse safely using a telescope.

Institute of Physics (IOP)

In 2025, I was the magnetosphere role model for the IOP’s Mimi’s Big Earth Adventure exhibition, helping to introduce young people to Earth science. Read more
